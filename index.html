Haoning Wu 「吴浩宁」

PhD Candidate @ SJTU·Top Talent Research Intern @ MiniMax

I am currently a final-year PhD candidate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), fortunately advised by Prof. Weidi Xie and Prof. Ya Zhang. Before that, I received my B.Eng. degree in Information Engineering (IEEE Pilot Class) also from SJTU in 2022.

I am generally interested in multi-modal learning, especially visual generative models and spatial intelligence. My long-term vision is to build Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) that surpasses human capabilities in perception, thinking, and action.

Previously, I have gained internship experience at Shanghai AI Laboratory and Alibaba (Amap CV Lab). And now, I am a Top Talent research intern at MiniMax (Hailuo), working on building omni-modal foundation models for MiniMax-H3.

Expected to graduate in Summer 2027, I am actively seeking opportunities as a research intern or full-time researcher for visual generative models, Omni-LLMs or embodied AI.

I am always open to discussions and collaborations, feel free to reach out.
