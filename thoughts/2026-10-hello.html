Hello, world: why I keep these notes

October 2026

Papers record what eventually worked. They rarely record how I was thinking along the way: the hunches, the doubts, and the questions I could not answer yet.

This corner of my homepage is for those. I plan to write short, informal notes from time to time, on research directions, lessons from projects, and occasionally things outside research, and to keep them as they were written, so that I can look back and see how my views change.

Opinions here are my own, and some of them will probably turn out to be wrong. That is part of the point.
